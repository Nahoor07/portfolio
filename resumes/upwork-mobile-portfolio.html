Project 01 · Ride-hailing · AI

Lumi — AI-Powered Ride-Hailing

Two production apps for Android and iOS — a customer app and a driver app sharing one real-time layer — with an AI assistant that books rides from a plain-language chat.

React Native CLIReduxGoogle Maps WebSocketsFirebaseTwilioLLM tool calling
  • AI chat assistant turns “I need a ride to the mall” into a confirmed booking — parses pickup and drop-off, quotes the fare, and dispatches
  • AI wired to the real booking pipeline via tool calling, so it uses the same validation as the manual flow
  • Customer app: booking, fare tiers with name-your-price, and live driver tracking
  • Driver app: registration, incoming offers with counter-offer, and live navigation
  • Firebase Auth, Twilio OTP, and Expo push notifications

Live on Google Play

Project 03 · Logistics · UK courier

Odda — Same-Day Courier App

An on-demand courier app for the UK, paired with the Intercargo freight portal: book a parcel, collect live quotes from nearby drivers, and follow it door to door.

React Native CLISignalRRedux JWT AuthREST APIsAngular
  • Real-time tracking over SignalR — driver location, ETAs, and status pushed as they happen
  • Live quotes: a booking fans out to nearby drivers and returns competing offers in seconds
  • Booking flow with detour stops, cargo details, and ASAP / at / by / between scheduling
  • Active, completed, and cancelled bookings, plus push notifications for every milestone
